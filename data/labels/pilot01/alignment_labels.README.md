# pilot01 alignment labels — provenance

**v2 is the only version. v1 was deleted and must not be reintroduced as a fallback.**

## Files

- `pilot01_slide_timeline_v2.csv` — **hand-made by the project owner while listening to
  the recording**: start/end per slide in mm:ss.
- `pilot01_pointing_windows.csv` — 10 windows where the speaker is referring to
  something visual on screen, with a target type (`diagram`, `table`, `graph`,
  `figure`, `graph_and_figure`, `external_demo`). **Outside a window the label is
  UNKNOWN, not negative.**
- `pilot01_alignment_labels_v2.csv` — derived per-segment truth for all 51 frozen audio
  segments, generated by `scripts/make_alignment_labels.py`. Regenerate after any
  re-segmentation; do not hand-edit.

## Rules used to derive per-segment labels

- A segment's true slide is the one covering the **majority of its duration**.
- **Build slides**: pages 10/11 and 17/18 share identical spans — one slide, two page
  numbers. The lower page goes in `true_slide`, the pair in `also_correct`, and scoring
  accepts either.
- Segments with >50% of their duration in the outro (19:00–22:51) get
  `true_slide=none`, `section=qa`.
- Segments starting or ending within **±3 s of any slide boundary** are `ambiguous`.
- A zero-length slide can never be the majority slide. **v2 has none** — the rule is
  kept for other decks.

## Accuracy

**Ear-labelled v2, approximate (±3 s), n=42 talk segments** (9 ambiguous) + 9 Q&A
segments. 26 of 27 pages shown — slide 27 never was — forming **24 distinct slides**.
Slide 24 is a live demo not present in the deck. The labelled sequence is monotonic
non-decreasing, independently confirming the assumption the alignment DP is built on.

## What v2 changed

v1 was a first pass: it inferred slide ends from the next slide's start, which produced
three phantom zero-length slides (9, 11, 18) and one overlap (slide 15/16). v2 resolves
all of it — slide 9 is a real 5 s, 10/11 and 17/18 are build slides, slide 16's start is
corrected, and the outro begins at 19:00 rather than 19:05.

Ambiguous segments fell 15 → 9 and DP exact accuracy rose 69.0% → 76.2% **with no code
change**. See the v1→v2 table in `reports/regression.md`: the aligner did not improve,
the measurement did.

## Superseded and removed

An earlier text-anchored label set (11 rows, matched against slide-unique content) was
deleted when the ear labels arrived. A fully automatic attempt was tried and discarded
before that: it mapped the opening title segment to slide 6 on the words *goal* and
*over*.
